Job Application Assistant

Installable Android app. Share any job post to it from another app to auto-fill it here, generate a tailored cover letter, then hand off to Gmail to attach & send.
⚠️ Browsers can't send SMTP email directly, and there's no way for a webpage to auto-attach a file into Gmail's compose window — both are browser security restrictions, not something this tool can bypass. To make it as fast as possible: picking your CV auto-extracts it immediately, sharing a job post from any app auto-fills it here, and clicking "Open Gmail Draft" both opens the pre-filled draft and auto-downloads your CV so it's sitting right there in your Downloads folder — just drag it in and hit Send.

💾 Your CV, OpenRouter key and model choice are saved on this device (localStorage) so you won't need to re-enter them next time. Nothing is sent anywhere except OpenRouter (for generation) and Gmail (when you open the draft). Use "Clear saved data" below if this device is shared with others.

1 Upload your CV

Text is extracted automatically the moment you pick a file — no button to click.

2 Job post & cover letter generation

Get one at openrouter.ai/keys. Used only in your browser, never stored or sent anywhere else.

3 Hand off to Gmail

Gmail will open in a new tab with the recipient, subject and cover letter filled in. Drag your CV file into the compose window to attach it, then hit Send yourself.
